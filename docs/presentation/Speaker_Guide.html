Demonstration

Use the independently verified evaluation installation and privately supplied access. Show a complaint, officer information request, citizen reply and recorded outcome. Show a permit with private evidence and verification. Use another account to demonstrate record denial. Show the outbox and local guide honestly where providers are disabled.

The retained evidence totals 267 assertions: 124 workflow/security, 13 fresh installation, 5 installer, 113 landing/dataset and 12 carousel fixture. These are recorded local checks, not users or a full browser or penetration test. Refer the panel to the test summary for remaining hosted MySQL, provider receipt, accessibility, workload and recovery acceptance.

Review and questions
