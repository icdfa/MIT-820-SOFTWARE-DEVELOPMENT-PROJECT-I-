# Citizen and staff user guide

## Citizen: create an account and sign in

Choose **Create account**. Enter your name, phone and email, create a password of at least 12 characters including letters and numbers, accept the privacy notice and submit. The portal opens your citizen dashboard immediately.

Check your mailbox for the verification message if email delivery is configured. Open its link and choose **Verify email address**. You can resend the message from Profile. Verification confirms your email; it does not verify your legal identity.

Use **Sign in** on later visits. If two-factor login is enabled, enter your authenticator code or an unused recovery code. **Forgot password** requests a one-hour reset link. If email does not arrive, contact the portal's published support contact.

## Citizen: submit a complaint

1. Open **Service directory**, search or filter and select a complaint service.
2. Read the requirements and target time, then choose **Start request**.
3. Enter a clear subject, full description, location, state and priority. Explain any urgency in the description.
4. Attach relevant evidence if available. PDF, JPG and PNG are accepted, at most 5 MB each and three files per upload.
5. Confirm the information is accurate and choose **Submit request**.
6. Keep the reference and private tracking code shown on the request. **Request receipt** opens a printable record.

**Save draft** retains the form without sending it to an officer. Required basic details must still be completed. A draft has no service deadline until submitted and stays private to its owner. Use **Complete draft** to edit and submit it.

The portal is not an emergency response service. Use the appropriate emergency channel for immediate danger.

## Citizen: apply for a permit

Choose a permit/licence from Service directory and read its document requirements. Complete the request and attach the supporting documents. A permit with listed document requirements must have at least one attachment before submission; the officer checks whether the evidence is sufficient.

The service authority sets any published fee. This portal does not collect payments or card details. Follow the department's authorised instructions if payment is required.

After approval, open **View approval** to print or save the application approval record as PDF. The record includes a public verification address. Demonstration installations clearly mark these records as having no legal effect.

## Citizen: track and respond

**My requests** offers subject/reference search, type/status filters and pages of records. Open a request to see its department, assigned officer, target date, documents and status history.

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| Draft | Saved privately; not yet submitted |
| Submitted | Received and routed for departmental review |
| In review | Officer is reviewing the request |
| Needs information | Read the officer message and reply with the missing details |
| Approved | Permit application approved in the workspace |
| Resolved | Complaint recorded as resolved |
| Rejected | Read the recorded decision and reason |
| Withdrawn | Owner withdrew the request before a final decision |

Reply to **Needs information** with a message and any additional documents. The request returns to the submitted queue. Other citizen-visible messages remain part of the record. Closed requests and drafts do not accept conversation replies.

Withdraw an eligible draft, submitted request or information request using the reason form. A request already in review cannot be withdrawn through this control. A resolved complaint can be reopened with a reason; the department receives a new target. Permits cannot be reopened through the complaint control.

After approval, resolution or rejection you can leave one rating and comment. Reopening a complaint clears the old feedback so the next outcome can be rated.

**Track a request** also works without signing in, using both the reference and private code. It shows limited status progress for ten minutes and excludes personal details, conversation text and documents. Keep the private code confidential.

## Citizen: notifications, assistance and security

**Notifications** keeps in-app updates even when email/SMS is unavailable. Mark all read when reviewed. Profile lets you update your name and phone, choose routine email/SMS preferences and change your password. Your login email is changed by an authorised account administrator.

The **Citizen assistant** offers a service guide based on published FAQs. If the operator has configured AI, the screen identifies AI assistance and offers a checkbox to use it. AI questions are sent to the provider with public service facts; avoid entering private documents, identity numbers or passwords. The assistant does not approve applications or inspect private case data. Use the request conversation for individual decisions.

In **Account security**, add the displayed key to a TOTP authenticator and confirm a current code. Save the eight recovery codes immediately; they are shown once. You can revoke other signed-in device sessions here. A password change signs out other sessions. Keep your device clock accurate for authenticator codes.

## Officer: review requests

Your workspace shows submitted requests in your department. Use **Assigned to me**, **Overdue queue** and filters to organise work. Department access is broader than assignment; permissions determine the actions available.

Open a request, read its details and download authorised supporting documents. Start with **In review** and record a clear update. If evidence is missing, choose **Needs information** and describe exactly what is needed. The citizen's reply returns it to Submitted. Begin review again before recording a final outcome.

A permit can be Approved or Rejected from review. A complaint can be Resolved or Rejected. The form requires an explanation. If another officer updates the record first, reload before saving your decision; version checks prevent overwriting that update.

Use Conversation to send a citizen-visible message. If authorised, select **Internal note** for staff-only content. Documents attached to an internal note have the same private visibility. Internal note permission is required both to see and to download them.

## Department head and administrator

Assignment selects an active, authorised reviewing officer in the request's department. Only roles with cross-department routing permission and organisation scope can change the department. Use **Unassigned** when no appropriate officer is available.

Reports show status totals, open overdue work, ratings and the share of closed decisions recorded within target. Exports are limited to records in your scope, up to 5,000 matching records per export. Use filters to narrow larger extracts. The auditor role can read submitted records and reports but has no decision or internal-note permission by default.

Administrators create staff accounts, maintain departments/services, manage permissions and configure integrations. Communications officers manage outbox, templates and public content without request access. Refer to OPERATIONS.md for those controls and backup procedures.
