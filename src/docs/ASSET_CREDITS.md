# Landing image credits

CivicFlow 1.0.3 · 19 October 2026

Three original service illustrations were generated for this portal using the built-in imagegen tool (not the CLI fallback). These images depict fictional people and service scenarios; they are not photographs of actual officials, government work or applicant businesses. No government seal or third-party logo is used in the new images.

Generated PNG originals were preserved. The project copies were converted to WebP at quality 82, without cropping or enlarging, for local delivery. No remote image/CDN request is required.

## citizen-services.webp

Final project asset: `assets/img/landing/citizen-services.webp`

Generation prompt:

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: wide 16:9 website image for a Nigerian digital government service portal carousel. Primary request: an editorial-style illustrative photograph of a welcoming modern Nigerian public service counter. A fictional West African female service officer in a navy office outfit assists a fictional West African male citizen in a light blue kaftan, with a thin plain document folder on the desk. Natural expressive interaction, realistic hands and facial features, calm professional office, subtle Nigerian architectural context, daylight and warm neutral materials. Composition: subjects and service desk mainly on the right half; left half quieter, slightly defocused neutral office interior suitable for dark overlay and white website text. Polished realistic photography, not a UI mockup. No text, no logos, no government insignia, no watermarks, no legible documents, no screens with readable personal data. This illustrates a service scenario and does not depict a real official or event.
